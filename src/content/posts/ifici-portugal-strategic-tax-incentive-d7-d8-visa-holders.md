## IFICI: The Tax Incentive That Attracts Digital Nomads and Investors to Portugal

![IFICI Portugal tax incentive](https://palegoldenrod-skunk-424266.hostingersite.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Imagem1.png)

Portugal has increasingly become a top destination for highly skilled professionals, global investors, and digital nomads. One of the key mechanisms enhancing this appeal is the IFICI (Tax Incentive for Scientific Research and Innovation), a special regime established under Article 58-A of the Portuguese Tax Benefits Statute (EBF), regulated by Ordinance No. 352/2024.

This article outlines the advantages of the IFICI clearly and strategically, especially for non-resident individuals seeking to establish residence in Portugal through the D7 or D8 visas.

## 1. What is the IFICI?

The IFICI offers a flat 20% personal income tax rate for 10 years to highly qualified professionals or entrepreneurs engaged in innovation or industrial sectors.

It applies to:

- Employees or independent professionals in industrial or tech-based companies
- Activities with high export potential
- Companies operating under the CAE codes listed in the annex of the Ordinance

## 2. Who can benefit from IFICI?

**This regime is ideal for:**

- Digital nomads (D8 visa) with international contracts and strong technical profiles
- Individuals with stable income seeking a better lifestyle in Portugal (D7 visa)
- Investors planning to establish companies in Portugal in the fields of innovation, technology, or export-oriented industries

## 3. Key conditions

- Becoming a tax resident in Portugal (more than 183 days/year or proof of habitual residence)
- Creating or working for a company with an eligible CAE code
- For companies: proving significant exports or obtaining recognition from IAPMEI or AICEP
- Official registration in the regime through Portuguese tax authorities or relevant entities

## 4. Benefits for D7 and D8 Visa Holders

- **D7:** May benefit from the IFICI if income is channeled through a Portuguese company engaged in qualified activities
- **D8 (Digital Nomad Visa):** Ideal for remote professionals with foreign clients. By establishing a business structure in Portugal, they can qualify under this tax regime

## 5. Practical application and fiscal strategy

- Setting up a company in Portugal can provide a solid pathway to IFICI eligibility
- Reinforces eligibility through local job creation or investment in fixed assets
- Requires a sound legal and fiscal framework from the moment of tax residency
